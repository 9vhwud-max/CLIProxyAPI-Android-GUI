# Third-party notes

This bundle contains or references the user's uploaded CLIProxyAPI source and Android/Termux port. Their original license files are preserved in their respective source directories.

The Android application depends at build time on Mozilla GeckoView. GeckoView/Firefox source is distributed under Mozilla's applicable open-source licenses (principally MPL 2.0); this project references the published Maven artifact rather than redistributing source here.

AndroidX and Material Components are consumed from their standard Maven artifacts and keep their upstream licenses/notices through the dependency distribution/build process.
